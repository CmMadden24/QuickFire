How it works

Learn teaches a few new facts a day, in order (the 13s first, then 14s and so on), and brings back the ones you are about to forget. Each fact needs two good recalls before it graduates, then returns after longer and longer gaps, never more than 7 days.

Speed counts. Under your target time is graded Instant, a little over is Good, much slower is Slow and comes back sooner. Mastered means a stable memory and an average under about 2 seconds.

Any miss, anywhere means you do not know it yet, so Test misses go onto the schedule. A weak spot clears only after good recalls on separate days, never from drilling in one sitting.

Drilling a miss means 2 to 4 correct answers, flipped and spaced, before you move on.

Your data saves on every answer on this device and syncs between devices while you are signed in. Copy code in this screen is a manual backup.
